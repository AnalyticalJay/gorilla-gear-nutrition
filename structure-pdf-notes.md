## Immediate structural implications noted from the PDF

The document introduces a broader catalogue model than the current eight-category-only structure. It uses **brand** as the top browse dimension and **sub-category** as the second dimension, while also showing a parallel **shop-navigation taxonomy** with Supplements, Nutrition, Peptides & Wellness, Peptide Pens, Accessories, Clothing, and Treats. Several current site groupings will therefore need a structured mapping exercise rather than a simple category rename.

The PDF also indicates that some brands carry mixed inventory families such as Injectable Oils, Orals, SARMs, Peptides, Pens, Wellness, Merchandise, Treats, and Nutrition. That means the implementation plan must decide whether the site should use one canonical product category plus a new subcategory layer, or a dual taxonomy with separate customer browse collections and internal regulatory/product-family classifications.

## Current catalogue audit as of 2026-09-01

The current schema stores each product with one `categoryId` and does not have a first-class `brandId` or `subcategoryId`. The current customer brand filter is inferred from product name, slug, and description text rather than a relational brand assignment. Its configured customer-facing brand vocabulary currently includes Ouroboros Labs, Keifei Pharmaceuticals, UPA Labs, Pharmaceuticals, Mactropin, SP Laboratories, HD Labs, and BP Pharma.

The active database currently contains exactly eight active categories: Clothing (2 products), Fat Loss (39), Healthcare (19), Injectables (245), Orals (268), Peptides (156), SARMs (39), and Supplements (13), for 781 active products. Older categories such as BLACK SERIES, GH & Peptides, HYBRID INJECTABLES, INJECTABLE OILS, ORAL ANABOLICS, Pens, and WEIGHTLOSS P are inactive rather than deleted.

The current inferred product-name prefixes are broader and less consistent than the PDF brand list. The largest current groups are NOVA (136), HD (74), UPA (70), Vmed (66), Pharma (65), Keifei (54), unprefixed (52), Cooper (42), PC (39), OT (36), P (29), KH (26), HU (25), NP (18), iPharma (7), OL (7), BP (7), PSY (5), DP (5), WP (4), XLR (2), plus single-product or ambiguous prefixes including Cardarine, Growth Hormone, Ligandrol/Anibolicum, Nutrobal, Ostarine, Testolone, and Tirzepatide 10mg.

The PDF and current database therefore differ structurally in three material ways: the PDF defines 21 named brands and nested subcategories; the database has only one category relation and inferred brands; and the current filter omits many target brands while including legacy/ambiguous values. The implementation must add relational brand and subcategory metadata, normalize existing prefixes through an explicit mapping table, and avoid deleting or deactivating products until each out-of-structure record is matched against the PDF and checked for carts, orders, images, and other dependencies.
