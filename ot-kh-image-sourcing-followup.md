# OT and KH Product Image Sourcing Follow-up

## Scope

The targeted follow-up refreshed all currently active product records without a primary image for the two unresolved prefixes:

| Prefix | Records reviewed | Result |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| OT | 33 | No verified official manufacturer or authorised distributor image source identified. |
| KH | 23 | No verified official manufacturer or authorised distributor image source identified. |
| **Total** | **56** | **No images assigned automatically.** |

## Research outcome

Each product was re-researched individually using its product name, formulation, and strength. The returned brand-level research did not identify a trustworthy official manufacturer website, contact, or authorised distributor for either OT or KH. Broader web checks for the claimed OT/Olympic Transformation identity and the KH brand did not yield a verifiable brand source suitable for copying product artwork.

## Decision

No OT or KH image was imported. This is intentional: assigning generic steroid packaging, reseller imagery, marketplace assets, or ambiguous lookalikes would create a material risk of showing the wrong packaging or incorrect strength. The 56 affected products remain in the catalogue without a primary image until a verified source is supplied.

## Recommended evidence for a follow-up import

Provide either an official manufacturer media pack, a supplier-authorised product catalogue, or an explicit authorised distributor URL for OT and KH. A branded product image folder supplied by the client would also allow direct high-confidence matching against the 33 OT and 23 KH product records.
