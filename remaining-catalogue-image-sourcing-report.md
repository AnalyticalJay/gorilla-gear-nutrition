## Excluded and unresolved work

The remaining 293 products were not assigned automatically. This includes products with no returned match, medium-confidence matches, research results not returned to the main workspace, direct image URLs that failed to download, and results from unvalidated retailers or marketplaces. Watermarked, inaccessible, generic, or uncertain artwork remains excluded to protect the catalogue’s premium presentation and product accuracy.

The following direct-source image candidates failed during this pass and remain unassigned: two Mounjaro KwikPen records and seven Nova records whose direct asset URLs returned a 404 or network failure. The `OT` and `KH` worklist result files were not returned to the main workspace and need a dedicated follow-up search.
