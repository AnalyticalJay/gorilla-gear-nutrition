# Mobile Alignment Verification

## Phone-width review — 375 × 812

The public home, product catalogue, customer account, and Admin Orders screens were reviewed after the mobile alignment pass.

| View | Verified result |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Hero copy, calls to action, membership benefits, collection-card copy, promotional modules, and utility benefits are centred on phone widths while returning to the editorial desktop alignment at the `sm` breakpoint. |
| Product catalogue | The heading, access notice, mobile filter control, product-card content, pricing, and add-to-quote actions are centred and remain full-width/touch-friendly. |
| Customer account | Account heading, member card, navigation controls, order status summary, account status, and profile fields are centred on phone widths. |
| Admin Orders | The existing operational layout remains scan-friendly and touch-target safe; quote data and approval/rejection actions retain purposeful left/right grouping rather than forced centring. |

Form labels, data-entry fields, detailed operational rows, and admin review controls intentionally keep practical text alignment where centring would reduce clarity or make data entry harder.
